How to Play

Tilt
Tip the top of the phone away from you to roll forward, and back toward you to reverse
‹ ›
Pan the camera. The katamari always rolls the way the camera faces, so you steer by panning while you tilt
DASH
Hold for a burst of speed. You need it to climb — a slope you crawl at will stop you
RECENTRE
Re-zero the tilt to however you are holding the phone now. Use it whenever you shift position
II
Pause. From there you can restart or quit

No motion sensor, or you declined the permission? On-screen ▲ and ▼ arrows appear in place of RECENTRE and drive the katamari instead. Everything else is identical.

W A S D
Roll the katamari, always relative to where the camera is looking (arrow keys work too)
Mouse
Swing the camera around
Shift
Dash — costs momentum control
Space
Whip around 180°
Enter
Tap to finish the stage early (once you have hit the goal). Hold it for a moment to abandon the run and go back to the title.
Q / E
Nudge camera left / right
Esc / P
Pause
M
Mute music
R
Restart the stage

Anything smaller than your katamari sticks to it. Every object you collect makes the ball bigger, which makes bigger things collectable. Long thin things can be picked up even when they look too big — they will make you roll strangely until you cover them up. Slamming into something huge at speed will shake objects loose.

The stage does not end when you hit the goal — it ends when the clock does, and the size you finish at is the size that counts. Once you have passed the goal you can press Enter to stop early and bank what you have. If you manage to roll up absolutely everything, the round closes itself.
